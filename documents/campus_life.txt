reviews
I commuted from Long Island via the LIRR and subway, which was not too bad except for when there were delays. This is a commuter school, so students were in and out and for me there was not much socialization. The New Building was clean and easy to maneuver. Haaren Hall is connected to the New Building. Most of my classes were in these two facilities, but I had a few in North Hall, which was older and dirtier but not as bad as it could have been. The New Building has the Jay Walk, which is a nice respite from the city setting. I think John Jay College is one of the best CUNY schools in terms of facilities and education. I received a scholarship which helped me immensely with tuition. My biggest con was not having a huge social circle just because of my and others' commutes.

    AlumMay 7 2023

John Jay college is a really diverse school, although it has Criminal Justice on their name they offer other majors and even motivate students to major in other things like English, psychology, forensic science etc. What I like about John Jay is the environment they have a really modern and open building which is nice to hang around and have lunch while completing assignments.
