About

CUNY John Jay College of Criminal Justice is a higher education institution located in New York County, NY. In 2023, the most popular Bachelors Degree concentrations at CUNY John Jay College of Criminal Justice were Criminal Justice - Law Enforcement Administration (1,353 degrees awarded), Forensic Psychology (537 degrees), and Criminology (204 degrees).

In 2023, 4,196 degrees were awarded across all undergraduate and graduate programs at CUNY John Jay College of Criminal Justice. 66% of these degrees were awarded to women, and 34% awarded men. The most common race/ethnicity group of degree recipients was hispanic or latino (1,765 degrees), 2.12 times more than then the next closest race/ethnicity group, white (833 degrees).

The median undergraduate tuition at CUNY John Jay College of Criminal Justice is $6,930, which is $−17,645 less than the national average for Masters Colleges and Universities ($24,575).

The enrolled student population at CUNY John Jay College of Criminal Justice, both undergraduate and graduate, is 47.8% Hispanic or Latino, 17.1% White, 16.8% Black or African American, 11.9% Asian, 2.5% Two or More Races, 0.326% American Indian or Alaska Native, and 0.274% Native Hawaiian or Other Pacific Islanders.

Students enrolled at CUNY John Jay College of Criminal Justice in full-time Undergraduate programs are most commonly Hispanic or Latino Female (33.6%), followed by Hispanic or Latino Male (17.6%) and Black or African American Female (10.5%).
