What Students Say

CUNY John Jay College of Criminal Justice offers students a diverse and supportive environment, with a strong emphasis on criminal justice education. Many reviews highlight the engaging professors who not only bring real-world experience to the classroom but also foster close relationships with students due to smaller class sizes. While the campus provides a vibrant atmosphere with various events and student organizations, some students note the challenges of being a commuter institution, leading to limited social interaction outside of classes. The college is praised for its practical learning opportunities, including internships, and the supportive services available for students, particularly those balancing academic and personal responsibilities. However, some students express a need for improvement in communication around logistical issues and for more accessible resources, particularly for internship and scholarship opportunities. Overall, John Jay is recognized for preparing students well and cultivating a culture of care and community.
